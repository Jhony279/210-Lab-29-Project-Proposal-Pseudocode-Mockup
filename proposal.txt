Project Overview:
My project is a Transit hub simulation that will try and monitor the flow of commuters
and vehicles going across cities over 25 time periods (Each time period represents 15 
minutes). The goal is to simulate how transit stations manage arriving, departing, and
emergency/maintenance vehicles during peak and off-peak cycles.

Implementations:
I'll use a std::map that will hold all any data related to the stations. It will use
the hub's name as a unique key to dynamically store and acces any station. Each key
holds an array with 3 lists inside that will each represent Incoming Trains, Outgoing
Trains, and Service/Maintenance Vehicles.

Simulated events:
The code will simulate the arrival and departure of trains, Random momonets where the
traffic of trains becomes high, and when a service unit may be blocking a platform 
which would then cause a delay in departures
